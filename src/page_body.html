What it fixes

  • Boxes you can't click. Eyebrows, sources, labels and dates show a frame and can be moved and deleted.
  • Locked design shapes. Card backgrounds, quote marks and other layout shapes become normal shapes on each slide.
  • Panels. Card, step and stat panels can be selected, resized and deleted, also on slides added with New Slide.
  • Panel numbers. Card, step and list numbers can be deleted. Blank numbers are filled in so they show when presenting.
  • Hidden boxes. Small boxes stuck under bigger ones come to the front. Photos under overlays can be selected.
  • Headers lined up. Titles sit the same distance under the eyebrow on every slide, cover eyebrows match, and agenda numbers sit centred over their red stroke. New slides follow too.

Agenda numbers and the footer brand line (Growth. Orchestrated. | 2X) stay fixed as part of the design.

How to use it

  1. Close the deck in PowerPoint.
  2. Drop it here and save the “(fixed)” copy.
  3. Open it and check a slide or two.
  4. Use it in place of the original. For SharePoint or OneDrive, upload it over the original; version history keeps the old one.
Files are fixed inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and your original file is never changed.
Works on decks and templates built on the 2X Interim template (v1). Safe to run more than once.
